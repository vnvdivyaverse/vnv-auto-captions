How VNV Auto Captions works

  1. Upload a video containing Telugu, English, or mixed speech.
  2. Choose Auto detect or select one language, then generate captions.
  3. Review the transcript and edit text or timings in the editor.
  4. Choose the caption style, colour, size and position.
  5. Export the video. Keep the tab open while it records playback.

The first transcription downloads the Whisper Tiny multilingual model. Recognition quality and speed depend on your device, audio clarity and available memory. Word highlighting is estimated within each phrase, not exact forced alignment.
